FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. If they have WhatsApp — which almost everyone in the UK already does — they can order or book. Scanning a QR code or tapping a link opens a chat with your business directly.

Is this only for restaurants?

No — restaurants are where we're starting, since that's our current pilot. The underlying platform is built around locations, availability, staff and WhatsApp conversations, not menus specifically, so it extends naturally to salons, clinics, repair services and other appointment- or order-based businesses.

Can the AI recommend or invent things you don't actually offer?

No — it only ever works from your actual, live catalogue and prices. If it can't confidently match what a customer's asking for, it asks a clarifying question rather than guessing.

What does it cost per message on WhatsApp's side?

WhatsApp's own messaging fees are a few pence per conversation, paid by the business — never your customers, who message for free exactly like texting a friend.

Can I run more than one location?

Yes — locations, hours, and catalogue availability are all managed per site from one account, with staff access scoped to the locations they actually work at.
