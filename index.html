FAQ

Questions restaurant owners actually ask

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. If they have WhatsApp — which almost everyone in the UK already does — they can order. Scanning a table QR code or tapping a link opens a chat with your business directly.

Can the AI recommend or invent menu items that don't exist?

No — it only ever works from your actual, live menu and prices. If it can't confidently match what a customer's asking for, it asks a clarifying question rather than guessing.

What does it cost per message on WhatsApp's side?

WhatsApp's own messaging fees are a few pence per conversation, paid by the business — never your customers, who message for free exactly like texting a friend.

Do I need a POS or till system already?

No — PingSorted runs standalone. Your menu, staff and orders live in the dashboard from day one.

Can I run more than one location?

Yes — locations, hours, tables and menu availability are all managed per site from one account, with staff access scoped to the locations they actually work at.
